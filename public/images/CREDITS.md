# Image credits

The photographs in `public/images/real/` are illustrative scenes made for this website. They show the kind of lesson each section describes, in a rural Bangladesh setting (green fields, palm trees, right-hand-drive cars, traffic keeping left).

They are **not** photographs of Taiyas Car Training Center, its cars, its trainers, or its students. Do not caption them as “our car” or “our student”.

Replace any file with the centre’s own photo by saving it under the same name (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.webp`).
